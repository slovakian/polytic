## How to read this

- **Type** uses the library's three Label types: **Ideology** (a full tradition that can fit a whole map,
  or be used as an Area Label for one region of it), **Thin** (a thin-centred tradition layered on a host
  ideology: Freeden 1998; Mudde 2004), and **Foundation** (a named position on a Foundation Dimension).
  "Ideology (area)" marks an Ideology whose natural scope is one region (economics, democracy, foreign
  policy), so it will usually be shown as an Area Label.
- **Parent** gives the family, or the parent Label when the entry is a child (shown as `→ parent`).
- **Central Dimensions** use the exact Dimension names from `dimension-taxonomy.md`, followed by the
  Pole the Label leans to. `(F)` marks a Foundation Dimension. Two to five are given; they are the
  *central* (core-concept) Dimensions, not every Position the Label takes. Where a tradition is
  internally split on a Dimension, the Dimension is either left out or marked "split".
- **Sources** are one or two standard references or primary texts. Family-level references are listed
  once per family to avoid repetition. Anything I could not confirm is marked _(verify)_.
- **Names.** Each Label is named as its adherents name it. Where the common name is mainly an outsider's
  or pejorative term, or the name itself is contested, the Flags column says so.
- **Dispassion.** Gists state each tradition's own self-understanding in the same register, whatever its
  conventional reputation (`principles.md` §1, §3). A gist reports what adherents hold; it is never
  Polytic's endorsement or verdict.

### Flags

| Flag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **R** | Distinct from the named neighbour mainly by **Reasons** (how it decontests concepts) rather than Positions. Allowed by the framework, but matching it relies on AI judgment of Reasons. |
| **GAP** | The locked Dimensions **cannot** place it distinctly; something central to it has no Dimension. Details in [Gaps](#gaps-what-the-locked-dimensions-cannot-place). |
| **MERGE?** | Probably too fine-grained for its own entry; suggested merge target given. |
| **NAME** | The name is contested, mainly used by outsiders, or pejorative; see note. |
| **REG** | A regional or national variant of a wider Label; owner to decide whether regional variants get entries. |
| **UMB** | Umbrella term. Useful as a family heading and parent; a person should usually be matched to a child. |
| **PART** | Particularist: what makes it distinct is *which* group or nation it centres. The Dimensions place its shape, not its object (see Gaps). |

## Gaps: what the locked Dimensions cannot place

None of these is a reason to add a Dimension automatically; the framework says the set changes only by
deliberate owner revision, and Off-map Beliefs exist for exactly these cases.

1. **Ethical egoism** (FD55; bears on LB5 Objectivism and A3 Stirnerian anarchism). The Foundation survey
   noted egoism vs. altruism and did not carry it into the set. Objectivism's defining Reason, that
   selfishness is a virtue, has no Position anywhere; it can be placed only through its political
   Positions (which match minarchism). Options: accept as Reasons; or add a Foundation Dimension
   ("Self-interest or others' interest").
2. **Centrism** (L14). Defined relative to a left–right axis that Polytic rules out computing. As a
   Label it can only be matched on Reasons ("I pick from both sides", "compromise is a value") plus
   moderate Pull. Recommend: keep as a Label defined by Reasons and Tempo of change, or drop and let the
   Result say "no Main Label fits".
3. **Particularist traditions** (flag PART: Zionisms, Hindutva, Black and white nationalism, Pan-Africanism,
   indigenous resurgence, stateless-nation nationalism). The Dimensions place their *shape* (heritage-based
   nationhood, sovereignty, recognition) but not *which* nation or group they centre. Two mirror-image
   nationalisms land in the same place. Recommend: the group is a Reasons field on the Label match,
   not a Dimension.
4. **Exit-based and competitive governance** (N13 neoreaction, T8 network state, LB3 anarcho-capitalism in
   part; charter cities, panarchism). "Exit over voice" is only partly a facet of Design vs. evolved
   order and Political authority. Partial gap.
5. **Universality of rights / civilisationism** (N12 Eurasianism, NW7 Asian values). Stress-test gap 9.
   Moral objectivity catches the metaethical core only loosely: a civilisationist can be a moral realist
   about his own civilisation's values. Partial gap.
6. **Transnational religious polity** (RL14 Salafi-jihadism; also Hizb ut-Tahrir-style caliphatism). Their
   aim is neither pole of Sovereignty vs. pooled governance: they reject the nation-state system in favour
   of a single religious polity. Partial gap; Reasons can hold it.
7. **Non-market, non-state coordination** (A5 anarcho-communism; also some council communism).
   Economic coordination's Poles are Public direction and Market coordination; a federated, moneyless
   commune economy is neither. Partial gap, echoing stress-test gap 7.
8. **Revolutionary internationalism** (M4 Trotskyism). Permanent revolution sits loosely on Compatriot
   priority and Foreign-policy aims. Partial; mostly Reasons.
9. **Rejection of civilisation / morphological freedom / metaphysical Tradition** (A9 primitivism, T1
   transhumanism, N5 Traditionalism, T6 Landian accelerationism). Their Positions are placeable; their
   central claims are partly Off-map. Partial gaps; acceptable.
10. **Theory of truth** (FD39 pragmatism). Grounds for belief does not capture "truth is what works".
    Partial gap.
11. **"Corrupt elite"** (TH1 populism). Empirical, so in Reasons by design (stress-test gap 8).

Several families also have **no settled Foundation label** for one Pole: Zero-sum or positive-sum, Expert
trust, Causes of fortune and Work have named *theories* (just-world belief, "limited good",
Protestant work ethic) but few self-identified positions. I left them out rather than invent labels.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Granularity.** Do children get their own entries (e.g. Stalinism under Marxism–Leninism, Alt-right
   under White nationalism), or are they Reasons variants of a parent? The merge list is the dial.
2. **Contested names to settle:** Neoliberalism (L9); the "social justice" left (CL5) — which name does
   the library use, given no settled self-name?; Stalinism (M3); Alt-right (N10); Christian nationalism
   (RL5); Race realism vs. hereditarianism (FD11); Gender-critical (F6); Nativism (FD10, clashes with the
   anti-immigration sense); Centrism (L14).
3. **Regional variants (REG).** Keep national variants (Gaullism, Peronism, Kemalism, Blue Labour, Mead's
   schools, Fabianism) as Labels, or fold them into their parents and mention them only in narrative?
   They help non-US/UK users recognise themselves, but crowd the library.
4. **Particularist Labels (PART).** Should the library hold one entry per nationalism (Zionism, Hindutva,
   Black nationalism, Scottish nationalism…) or a few generic Thin nationalisms with the group recorded in
   Reasons? Gap 3 suggests the latter is more consistent with how matching works.
5. **Area Labels for economic and democratic doctrines (§14, §15).** Are Keynesianism, MMT, deliberative
   democracy etc. Labels, or Reasons vocabulary? The framework already says models of democracy are
   Reasons.
6. **Historical traditions with few living adherents** (divine-right absolutism, guild socialism, utopian
   socialism, Social Credit, Integral nationalism, Mohism, Legalism). Keep for Reference Figures, which
   are encoded the same way as users, even if few users match?
7. **Religions as Foundation Labels.** Theism is one entry; should Catholic, Protestant, Sunni, Shia,
   Buddhist etc. be Foundation Labels, or only appear through political-religious traditions?
8. **Gaps 1, 4 and 6** (egoism, exit, transnational religious polity) are the strongest candidates for
   a future Dimension revision, if the owner wants one.
9. **Violent strategies.** Salafi-jihadism, insurrectionary anarchism and Fanonism are included because
   the Dimension set maps Force in domestic politics like any other. Confirm that Labels for these are
   in scope on the same footing (the owner's stated direction suggests yes).
10. **Reference Figures.** Many entries were included because a likely Reference Figure fits them
    (Rand, Kropotkin, Hazony, Yarvin, Ghannouchi). The Figure catalog may change which Labels are worth
    keeping.
