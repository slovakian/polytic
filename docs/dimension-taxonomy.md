# Dimension taxonomy (source-of-truth asset)

The dimension taxonomy is Polytic's deliberate model of political space — the axes we
measure people on. It is the direct answer to "the compass has too few, too crude
axes." It is authored intentionally by the project owner and versioned in this repo;
it powers the grilling skill, the future app, and eventually the alignment math.

## The model

- **Curated and fixed (the skeleton).** A deliberate set of dimensions, each with named
  sub-topics and seed issues. Not emergent, not statistically derived — chosen.
- **Adaptive within (the flesh).** The grilling follows these dimensions but probes
  whatever issues surface underneath them (see `grilling-protocol.md`).
- **Why fixed matters.** A shared, stable skeleton is what makes profiles and
  "compare yourself to thinkers" possible later — you cannot align two people who were
  measured on different axes.

## Structure each dimension should carry

- A name and a crisp definition of what the axis captures.
- Sub-topics / facets within it.
- Seed issues the grilling can open with.
- (Future) how positions on it map toward labels in `label-library.md`.
