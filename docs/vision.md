# Vision

## The problem

Most people do not have clean, well-articulated language for their own politics. What they have is a bundle of moral intuitions, lived priorities, inherited slogans, and unresolved tensions — forced into cartoonish boxes that flatten almost everything that matters:

1. **Crude coordinate systems.** Left vs. right collapses economic organization, state authority, cultural tradition, nationalism, epistemology, and institutional change into a single line. The 2D political compass adds one axis and still reduces an entire philosophy to two numbers.
2. **Shallow static quizzes.** Fixed Likert-scale questionnaires cannot ask a follow-up, cannot tell how a person defines a contested word like "freedom" or "fairness," cannot probe a contradiction, and cannot separate a deeply held conviction from a passing impression or genuine ambivalence.
3. **Biased, safety-hedged AI.** Out-of-the-box language models carry the ideological skew of urban progressive norms and RLHF safety training. When asked about politics, they lecture, scold, shut down heterodox views, or engage in anxious "both-sides" whataboutism and false equivalence rather than rigorous, objective analysis.

## The core thesis

Polytic helps people understand their own politics in high resolution — acting with the demeanor of a **stoic, friendly academic or clinician**.

Instead of a static quiz or a preachy chatbot, Polytic interrogates and tests a person's political worldview with clinical objectivity: probing _why_ they hold a position, using concrete real-world policy dilemmas as diagnostic stress-tests to see where their principles collide, and naming both their settled convictions and their unresolved tensions.

## The archetypal user and the "aha!" moment

Polytic is built first for the **reflective, politically curious, or cross-pressured individual** who feels flattened by mainstream partisan binaries ("left vs. right", "Democrat vs. Republican") and unsatisfied by two-minute internet badges. They are willing to think hard and have their coherence tested because the payoff is two-fold:

1. **The Personal Mirror:** Seeing their own scattered intuitions, value trade-offs, and internal tensions articulated back to them with surgical accuracy — often better than they could state them themselves.
2. **The Intellectual Bridge:** Discovering what their ideas are actually called in the centuries-long tradition of political philosophy and political science — learning the established vocabulary, thinkers, and conceptual fault lines they are standing on, in plain language without academic pedantry.

## What Polytic measures

- **Enduring political philosophy, not electoral horse-races.** Polytic maps a person's underlying political worldview — their values, institutional principles, conceptual commitments, and epistemic foundations. Concrete policy flashpoints are used strictly as diagnostic instruments to test where abstract principles break or hold in practice, never to endorse a party, candidate, or ballot measure.
- **Globally portable skeleton, locally contextualized flesh.** The underlying conceptual framework and political-science vocabulary are universal across political thought, while the concrete policy stress-tests adapt dynamically to the country or political context the user inhabits.

## Product lifecycle: one-shot hook to living profile

Polytic combines the immediate gratification of a standalone diagnostic with the cumulative depth of a persistent personal profile:

1. **Zero-wall first session:** A new user enters directly into their first grilling session with zero signup wall and receives a complete **Result** at the end — including their anchored political labels, narrative synthesis, and their **Closest Thinkers** (matched against a curated catalog of historical and contemporary figures, highlighting both where they align and their primary point of divergence).
2. **Claiming a Living Profile:** At the end of the first session, the user can optionally create an account to save their result as a **Living Profile**. Profiles are **private by default** so users never self-censor during grilling, with a clear prompt encouraging them to make their profile public or share their Result link.
3. **Iterative deepening and belief freshness:** Returning users do not retake the test from scratch. Follow-up sessions pick up where they left off — zooming into specific topics, testing new real-world dilemmas, or challenging parts of their map. When a user's belief changes or contradicts an earlier session, Polytic preserves the superseded view as a **stale belief** in their history while updating their **current belief** so agent lookups and profile views always reflect their freshest thinking by default.

## North-star arc

Polytic grows in three concentric rings (and three corresponding rollout stages), where each ring depends on the fidelity of the one inside it:

1. **Ring 1 / Stage 1 — Personal Political Self-Understanding (Current Focus):** Solo grilling, the Result (with political labels and Closest Thinkers from a hand-encoded reference catalog), shareable Result links, and the optional Living Profile (private by default, encouraged to go public).
2. **Ring 2 / Stage 2 — High-Resolution Comparative Mapping:** Direct profile-to-profile comparison with friends and public profiles, plus agent-researched expansion of the Reference Figure catalog — showing not just agreement scores, but the exact root premises where two worldviews converge or diverge.
3. **Ring 3 / Stage 3 — Structured Deliberative Discourse:** Host structured, high-signal political discussions and debates between mapped profiles across divides, coordinated by a clinically neutral arbiter that focuses participants on their actual root disagreement rather than tribal slogans.
