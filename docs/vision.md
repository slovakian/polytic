# Vision

## The problem

People reach for cartoonish maps of their own politics. Left vs. right is a single
axis that flattens almost everything that matters. The political compass adds one
more axis and feels a little less silly — but it's still two crude numbers standing
in for a person's entire political philosophy. Worse, most political questionnaires
are built with statistical techniques on fixed questions: they can't ask a follow-up,
can't probe a contradiction, can't tell the difference between a confident position
and a vibe. They afford no nuance, and so they are not deep.

## The bet

AI changes what's possible. A capable model can _grill_ you — interview you,
challenge the coherence of your answers, reason with you about edge cases — and from
that conversation put words to your politics with a fidelity no static quiz can reach.
For the first time, someone can sit with an agent and have it genuinely articulate
_what they actually believe_, then hand them the established vocabulary that describes
it.

Polytik replaces "pick your quadrant" with a guided act of self-understanding.

## What a user gets

You answer a battery of probing questions across a deliberately-authored set of
political dimensions. The AI challenges weak spots — _"why this and not that?"_ — and
together you converge on a picture. At the end it delivers a **verdict**: the
political labels that best describe you (drawn from the long tradition of
political-science terminology), plus a narrative that captures the nuance those labels
miss.

The thrill is the moment of recognition — an AI putting precise words to something you
only half-had language for.

## North star

Polytik grows from a personal tool into a social space for political
self-understanding:

- **Profiles** — save your verdict and the reasoning behind it.
- **Alignment** — see how closely your politics match specific thinkers, politicians,
  and online personalities, measured on a shared vocabulary.
- **Sharing & community** — public profiles, shareable grilling sessions, public
  prompts people respond to.
- **Discourse** — AI-arbitrated asynchronous discussions, and eventually live debates,
  coordinated by a neutral arbiter.

The ordering and detail of these live in `roadmap.md`. The principles that keep all of
it honest live in `principles.md`.
