### Reasons

**Self-understanding.** Human history is governed by an eternal, merciless law of nature:
the struggle for existence among races. In this struggle, the Aryan race represents the sole
creative force of human civilization, the founder of culture. The state is not an economic
organization, a legal abstraction, or an end in itself; it is a racial organism whose sole
justification is the preservation, physical purification, and territorial expansion of the
racial stock.

Liberalism, democracy, and Marxism are suicidal doctrines that deny nature's aristocratic
principle. Liberalism dissolves the collective racial body into selfish, hedonistic individuals;
parliamentary democracy substitutes cowardice and majority rule for personal genius and
responsibility; Marxism artificially divides the blood community along artificial class lines
to subjugate it to cosmopolitan finance capital.

To survive, the nation must be restructured along racial and aristocratic lines under the
unquestioned authority of a Leader (_Führer_). The economy must serve national defense and
autarky rather than private international speculation. Internally, the nation must be purged
of racial pollutants and ideological subversion; externally, it must secure the necessary
_Lebensraum_ (living space) by the sword, ensuring that the German people possess the soil
required to sustain their demographic growth and civilizational dominion.

**Key concepts.**

- **Racial hierarchy & purity:** The doctrine that humanity is divided into distinct,
  unequal biological races, with the Aryan at the apex; miscegenation leads inevitably to
  cultural decay.
- **_Volksgemeinschaft_ (People's Community):** An organic, classless national community
  united by common blood and collective sacrifice, superseding individual rights and class
  divisions.
- **_Führerprinzip_ (Leader Principle):** Hierarchical governance where supreme authority
  resides in the Leader, requiring total obedience from below and absolute responsibility
  from above.
- **_Lebensraum_ (Living Space):** The geopolitical imperative to conquer agrarian land and
  resources in Eastern Europe to ensure the physical survival and demographic expansion of the
  German race.
- **Anti-universalism:** Complete rejection of universal human rights, Christian egalitarianism,
  and international legal constraints as artificial contrivances that defy nature's law of
  survival of the fittest.
