## White nationalism

- **Type:** Ideology
- **Parent:** —
- **Also called:** White advocacy, racial nationalism, White separatism (for its
  separatist form); critics: "White supremacy" (a distinct claim; see _Telling it
  apart_)
- **Variants:** Alt-right
- **Centered group:** people of European descent, across national borders

### Reasons

**Self-description.** Peoples of European descent form a distinct extended family,
bound by shared ancestry and a shared civilizational inheritance. Like every other
people, they have a legitimate interest in their own survival and in homelands where
they remain the majority and set the culture. Mass immigration and low birth rates are
turning them into minorities in the countries their ancestors built, and they should be
free to say so and to organize to prevent it, as other groups already do.

**Key concepts.**

- **Nation:** a people of common descent, not the holders of a passport; citizenship
  should follow peoplehood.
- **Self-determination:** the right of a people, not only of individuals, to a homeland
  and to its own future, applied to Europeans as to anyone.
- **Equality:** for its separatist majority, peoples are different and each is entitled to
  its own space; for a supremacist minority, peoples are ranked (see _Telling it
  apart_).
- **Diversity:** a source of conflict, distrust, and the dilution of a people, rather than
  a strength.

**Signature reasons.**

- "Every people has a right to its own homeland, and that includes White people."
- "A nation is a people, not a passport."
- "Demographic change is replacing the historic majority, and nobody asked us."
- "Other groups are allowed ethnic solidarity. We are told ours is evil."
- "Racial differences are real, and they matter for how societies work." (its
  hereditarian strand)

**Centered group.** People of European descent as one pan-national group, rather than
any single nation such as the French or the Hungarians.

**Telling it apart.**

- **Ethnic nationalism:** centers one particular nation (Hungarians, Serbs); White
  nationalism centers a race that spans nations (centered group).
- **National populism:** also restricts immigration, but defines the nation by culture
  and citizenship and accepts non-White fellow citizens (_Basis of nationhood_, the
  central Dimension).
- **Identitarianism:** rooted in regional and national European identities and framed
  ethnoculturally, mostly avoiding biological race (centered group; _Race and
  ethnicity_; Reasons).
- **National Socialism:** adds a totalitarian one-party state, a leader principle, and
  political violence (_Leadership_, _Pluralism vs. unity_, _Force in domestic
  politics_).
- **"White supremacy":** strictly, the claim that White people should rule over other
  races (_Social hierarchy_, _Hierarchy or equality_). Most self-described White
  nationalists are separatists and reject it; a person who holds it is described by those
  Dimensions, not by this Label's name alone.
- **Black nationalism:** the same shape, centering people of African descent (centered
  group).

**Sources.** Jared Taylor, _White Identity_ (2011); Greg Johnson, _The White

in America_ (2003); George Hawley, _Making Sense of the Alt-Right_ (2017); Cas Mudde,
_The Far Right Today_ (2019).
